Move the mouse over the logos at the bottom of each footer: a circle of light follows it. The logo is written once; the script lays a lit copy over it and opens it only around the pointer — and keeps it there while the page scrolls. On a touch screen there is no pointer, so no light: a logo is hidden there unless it asks to stay (data-pg-spotlight-touch) — here all stay but Gaga's. Each footer says which. The first one has every option, to try.

Bugatti — dark until found, edge to edge · shown on phones

            

                As bugatti.com's footer has it: --pg-spotlight-base: 0, so nothing shows without the
                light — but the light is as wide as the logo and fades all the way (--pg-spotlight-size:
                0%, --pg-spotlight-fade: 60%, at --pg-spotlight-lit: 0.4), so the
                letters only fade out the farther they are from the pointer. Or a small circle, still dark around it. On a
                phone it stays at 0.2 — so a logo that is dark until found is not lost there.
